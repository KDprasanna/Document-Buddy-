Aadhaar Services

Information about commonly required documents and steps for Aadhaar-related services.

📄 Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card or Aadhaar number
  • Proof of Identity, if required
  • Proof of Address, if required
  • Passport-size photograph, if required
  • Mobile number for OTP verification
  • Supporting documents for the requested service

👤 Who Can Apply?

Aadhaar-related services can be requested by Aadhaar holders. The required documents and process may vary depending on the service you need.

📝 Step-by-Step Process

  1. Identify the Aadhaar service you need.
  2. Check the required documents for that service.
  3. Visit the appropriate Aadhaar service centre or use an official online service when available.
  4. Submit the required information and documents.
  5. Complete verification or biometric authentication if required.
  6. Keep the acknowledgement or reference number safely for future tracking.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Providing incorrect personal information
  • Using outdated supporting documents
  • Entering an incorrect mobile number
  • Not checking the acknowledgement details
  • Sharing OTPs or Aadhaar details with unknown people
