Scholarship Application

Understand the documents and steps commonly required when applying for a scholarship.

📄 Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Recent Passport-size Photograph
  • Previous Academic Marksheets
  • Income Certificate, if required
  • Caste / Category Certificate, if applicable
  • Bank Account Details
  • College / Institution Certificate

👤 Who Can Apply?

Students who satisfy the eligibility conditions of the particular scholarship can apply. Eligibility may depend on academic performance, family income, category, course, institution, or other conditions.

📝 Step-by-Step Process

  1. Check the scholarship eligibility.
  2. Collect the required documents.
  3. Register on the relevant scholarship portal.
  4. Fill in the application form carefully.
  5. Upload the required documents.
  6. Review the application before submitting.
  7. Save the application or acknowledgement number.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Entering incorrect personal information.
  • Uploading unclear documents.
  • Using an expired certificate when a current certificate is required.
  • Missing the scholarship application deadline.
  • Not checking the application status.
