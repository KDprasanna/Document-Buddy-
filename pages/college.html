College Admission

Find the documents and steps required for college admission.

📄 Required Documents

  • 10th Class Certificate
  • 12th / Intermediate Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Admission / Application Form

👤 Who Can Apply?

Students who have completed the required educational qualification and satisfy the eligibility conditions of the college or course can apply.

📝 Step-by-Step Process

  1. Check the eligibility requirements.
  2. Select the college and course.
  3. Collect the required documents.
  4. Complete the admission application.
  5. Submit the required documents.
  6. Complete the admission process.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Submitting incorrect information.
  • Forgetting required certificates.
  • Uploading unclear documents.
  • Missing the application deadline.
