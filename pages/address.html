🏠 Address Change

Understand the commonly required documents and steps for updating an address.

📄 Required Documents

  • Valid identity proof
  • Proof of the new address
  • Aadhaar Card, if applicable
  • Utility bill, bank statement or other accepted address proof
  • Passport-size photograph, if required
  • Application form for the relevant service

👤 Who Can Apply?

A person who needs to update their residential or correspondence address can apply. The exact requirements depend on the service or organisation.

📝 Step-by-Step Process

  1. Identify the document or service where the address needs to be changed.
  2. Check which address-proof documents are accepted.
  3. Fill out the appropriate address-change application.
  4. Submit the required documents and new address details.
  5. Complete verification if required.
  6. Keep the acknowledgement or reference number for tracking.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Entering the new address incorrectly
  • Submitting an address proof with mismatched details
  • Using an expired or unacceptable document
  • Forgetting to check the application before submitting
  • Not keeping the acknowledgement safely
