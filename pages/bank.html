🏦 Bank Account Opening

Understand the commonly required documents and steps for opening a bank account.

📄 Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card or other officially accepted identity proof
  • Address proof, if required
  • PAN Card or Form 60, as applicable
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Mobile number
  • Additional documents requested by the bank, if applicable

👤 Who Can Apply?

Individuals who meet the bank's account-opening requirements can apply. Requirements may vary depending on the type of account and the bank.

📝 Step-by-Step Process

  1. Choose the type of bank account you want to open.
  2. Check the bank's document and eligibility requirements.
  3. Fill in the account-opening application form.
  4. Submit the required documents for verification.
  5. Complete identity verification or KYC as required.
  6. Receive your account details after successful verification.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Entering incorrect personal information
  • Submitting expired or invalid documents
  • Providing an incorrect mobile number
  • Not checking the application before submitting it
  • Sharing banking passwords, PINs or OTPs with others
